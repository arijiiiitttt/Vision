# 3D models used by the Real world

Geometry by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), taken from the free Kenney starter kits on GitHub:
- KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-City-Builder: houses (house-*.glb), trees (tree.glb), street lamp (lamp.glb)
- KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Racing: cars (racing/car-*.glb)

Kenney's assets are public domain (CC0) according to kenney.nl; the starter-kit repositories are MIT licensed
(see LICENSE-Kenney-starter-kits.txt). tree.glb and lamp.glb are the original models with the ground tile / second post removed.
No credit is required, but thank you, Kenney.
